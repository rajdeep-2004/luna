Palette

Warm, lamplit and dark, because Luna is played at night, on the couch, after work. Amber means "go" (buttons, XP, the lantern). Vermilion is the seal: correct answers, the active tab, the winner. It is never used for body text.

Coming home

On opening the app. The shoji slide apart onto the night and the moon, then the den settles.

Rules that keep it from turning into slop again

    Do

  • One line of copy per element. Objects and numbers before words.
  • One amber action per screen. Everything else is secondary.
  • Build scenes from 3–4 flat paper layers with real depth (offset shapes), lit from one warm source.
  • Give unequal things unequal weight: the boss fight gets a stage, the kata gets the table.
  • Every press reacts: the icon sinks 2 pt, a haptic fires, nothing ever ripples grey.

    Don't

  • No cards on the main screens, and never a card inside a card.
  • No progress bars or percentages. The ensō, the lantern and stamps carry progress.
  • No glows, gradients or neon. Light is a flat, lighter paper shape.
  • No emoji as icons, and no generic icon set. Every icon is a cut-paper object from the den.
  • No paragraphs outside the answer explanation, and that one starts collapsed.
